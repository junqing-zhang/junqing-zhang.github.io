University of Liverpool has been one of the leading institutions worldwide in the area of _key generation from wireless channels_. We have carried out systematic research to investigate different aspects of this exciting technique, ranging from theoretical modelling, simulation, and experimental validations. This page summarises our key generation publication.

Please find a [Poster]({{ site.url }}/files/pdf/PosterKeyGenLiv.pdf){:target="_blank"} that summarizes our work.
