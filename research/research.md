My research focuses on developing innovative physical-layer security and wireless sensing solutions for future wireless and IoT systems. It aims to bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical implementation through advanced signal processing, machine learning, and extensive experimental validation using state-of-the-art wireless testbeds and equipment.

<h2 class="research-title" id="radio-frequency-fingerprint-identification">Radio-Frequency Fingerprint Identification</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Manufacturing variations give the components of wireless devices slightly different characteristics, such as oscillator frequency offsets. Like biometric fingerprints, these characteristics can distinguish devices. An RFFI system enrols known device fingerprints and subsequently identifies a transmitting device by comparing its signal with the stored fingerprints.</p>

<h2 class="research-title" id="key-generation-from-wireless-channels">Key Generation from Wireless Channels</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Key generation from wireless channels exploits the randomness and reciprocity of wireless propagation to establish shared secret keys between communicating devices.
